💡 HUD Tips

1
Two-finger layout

Keep the layout simple and avoid placing important buttons too close together.

2
Three-finger layout

Try using your index finger for firing while your thumbs handle movement and camera aim.

3
Four-finger layout

Separate actions across fingers only if you can press them consistently without strain.

There is no single best HUD for everyone. Comfort and repeatability matter more than copying a pro player's layout.

Practice Routine

Headshot Training Guide

Practice accuracy before chasing speed. Change only one setting at a time.

1
Warm up for 3–5 minutes

Use the Training Ground to get comfortable with your current sensitivity.

2
Start with chest-level aim

Place your crosshair around the upper chest before attempting a controlled upward drag.

3
Use a short, controlled drag

Drag gently upward while firing. Avoid swiping so far that the crosshair passes over the target.

4
Review your misses

If your aim passes above the head, reduce General or Red Dot slightly. If it stays too low, increase one setting slightly.

5
Repeat consistently

Test the same weapon and distance for several minutes before changing your profile.

🎯 Quick Adjustment Guide

↑
Aim keeps going above the head

Lower General or Red Dot by 3–5 points, then test again.

↓
Aim stays below the head

Raise General or Red Dot by 3–5 points, then test again.

↔
Aim feels shaky

Try a smaller drag, a more comfortable fire-button size, or slightly lower sensitivity.

✓
Results feel consistent

Keep the profile unchanged for a few matches before making more adjustments.

These are general practice tips, not guaranteed outcomes. Performance also depends on FPS, latency, weapon, distance, and practice.
