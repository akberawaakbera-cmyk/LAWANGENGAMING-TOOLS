🔥 Headshot Training Guide

A simple routine to develop aim control and consistency.

01

Warm Up Your Aim

Spend 3–5 minutes tracking targets and getting comfortable with your sensitivity.

02

Practice Crosshair Placement

Keep your crosshair near the expected target height instead of always aiming at the ground.

03

Practice Controlled Drag

Use small, controlled finger movements. Avoid making every movement excessively fast.

04

Test One Change at a Time

If aim feels too slow or too fast, adjust one setting slightly and test again.

05

Review Your Consistency

Focus on repeatable aim and positioning rather than expecting every shot to be a headshot.
